FluidR3_GM sampled instruments
================================

CadenceFlow includes pre-rendered MP3 samples generated from FluidR3_GM.sf2 and distributed by the MIDI.js Soundfonts project:

https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts/tree/044fab8e1456bfafc5776e86dfd6bb8697149aef/FluidR3_GM

Pinned upstream revision: 044fab8e1456bfafc5776e86dfd6bb8697149aef

The MIDI.js Soundfonts project identifies these FluidR3_GM assets as licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. A copy of the license is included in FluidR3_GM-CC-BY-3.0.txt.

The application loads only the local copies committed with CadenceFlow. It does not fetch instrument samples from the project website at runtime.

The committed local sample maps contain all 128 General MIDI programs. The
program-to-file mapping, file sizes, and SHA-256 digests are recorded in
public/audio/melody/manifest.json and verified by the offline asset checker.
