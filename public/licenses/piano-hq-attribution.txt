ATTRIBUTION SUMMARY
-------------------
Salamander Grand Piano V3 was recorded by Alexander Holm using a Yamaha C5 grand
piano at 48kHz, 24-bit resolution with two AKG C414 microphones in an AB stereo
configuration placed ~12cm above the strings.

The original work features 16 discrete velocity layers sampled in minor thirds
from A0 (MIDI pitch 21) through C8 (MIDI pitch 108).

CADENCEFLOW DERIVATIVE NOTICE
-----------------------------
CadenceFlow includes web-ready derivative audio assets and a companion metadata
manifest prepared specifically for low-latency in-browser WebAudio playback.

CadenceFlow performs the following transformations on the source recordings:
1. Encoding: Source recordings are prepared as Ogg Vorbis at 48kHz stereo for
   in-browser playback.
2. Organization: Preserves all 16 discrete velocity layers across all 30 sampled
   minor-third root pitches (480 sample regions total).
3. Metadata: Strips extraneous DAW headers and generates a structured,
   lightweight JSON manifest (`manifest.json`) for lazy-loaded velocity-sensitive
   WebAudio playback.
